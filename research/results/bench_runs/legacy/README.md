# Archived unqualified benchmark manifests

These five JSON files were present in the active `bench_runs` directory before their workloads were rerun. They are preserved byte-for-byte here for historical provenance and are excluded from `benchmarks.manifest.read_all()` and the scoreboard. Their SHA-256 digests and qualification reasons are recorded in `index.json`.

Current active manifests live one directory above and are regenerated by their registered scripts from a clean checkout. Historical host measurements and full source commits were not captured; this archive does not infer or reconstruct them.
